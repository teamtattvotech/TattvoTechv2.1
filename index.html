Our Process

How We Work

A simple, written process so you always know what is happening, what comes next, and what has been agreed.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We understand the business problem, your requirements and any technical constraints.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    You receive a written scope with deliverables, timeline, price and payment milestones.

  3. 03

    Development

    We build the agreed solution and share progress at the checkpoints set out in the proposal.

  4. 04

    Testing

    We check functionality and responsive behaviour across the agreed browsers and devices.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We deploy the work or transfer code, files and access, as agreed in the proposal.

  6. 06

    Support

    Additional maintenance or improvements can be provided under a separately agreed arrangement.

Pricing & Engagement

Clear Pricing, Agreed Up Front

Every project is different, so we don't publish one fixed price list. Pricing depends on the project scope, technical requirements and delivery commitments.

Agreed in writing before work begins

  • Scope and deliverables: what will be built, and what is not included.
  • Timeline and milestones: checkpoints where you review progress.
  • Price and payment terms: project-based pricing with milestone payments.
  • Testing, handover and support: exactly what is included, and how further work is handled.

Small projects are welcome

A well-defined small task is a good way to start working together. Typical examples:

  • Landing pages

  • Custom development tasks
