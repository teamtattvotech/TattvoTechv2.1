Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information TATTVO TECH ("we", "us") collects through tattvotech.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. TATTVO TECH is an MSME (Udyam) registered technology services business based in India.

1. Information we collect

Information you send us. When you submit the project inquiry form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, company or agency name, project type, approximate budget, preferred contact method, phone or WhatsApp number, and your project description. Only name, email, project type and project description are required. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the information you share through that channel.

Analytics information. If analytics cookies are allowed, Google Analytics collects information about how the website is used, such as pages viewed, approximate location (country or city level), device and browser type, referring website, and interactions such as clicks on contact buttons. We do not send your form contents, name, email or phone number to Google Analytics.

Technical information. Our hosting provider and the content delivery networks that serve the website's code and styles may process your IP address and browser details to deliver pages securely.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to your inquiry and discuss your requirements.
  • To prepare proposals and, if we work together, to deliver and support the agreed project.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it.
  • To protect the website and form against spam and misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not add inquiry contacts to marketing mailing lists without asking first.

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process inquiry details because you have asked us to respond (taking steps before entering into a contract) and on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries. Analytics cookies are used on the basis of consent where the law requires it.

3. Service providers

We use a small number of third-party services to run the website. They process information on our behalf or as part of providing their service:

  • Formspree: receives and forwards inquiry form submissions to our business inbox.
  • Google Analytics (Google): website usage statistics, subject to your cookie choice.
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting.
  • Public content delivery networks (such as cdnjs, jsDelivr and the Tailwind CSS CDN): deliver the website's scripts, styles and icons.
  • Email and messaging providers: used when we correspond with you by email or WhatsApp.

Some of these providers are located outside India and outside your country, including in the United States. By using them, your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries under the providers' own safeguards.

4. Cookies and local storage

With your permission, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) to distinguish visitors and sessions. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics cookies stay off unless you accept them. Visitors elsewhere can decline them at any time.

We store your choice in your browser's local storage (under tt_analytics_consent) so we don't ask again on every visit. We do not use advertising cookies.

You can change your choice at any time: . You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry details only for as long as we need them to respond, manage any resulting project, and meet legal, tax or accounting obligations. Inquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted once they are no longer needed. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting in our Analytics account.

6. Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are sent over an encrypted connection. Access to inquiry information is limited to the people who need it to respond. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information you share.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email info@tattvotech.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
