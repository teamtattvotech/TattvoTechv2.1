TATTVO VISION

Reimagining how people understand their financial information.

We're exploring a specialized AI assistant designed to help people understand accounting, tax, GST, and financial information — with evidence behind the answers.

Interactive Prototype: Experience simulated workflows below. We are validating this product vision before final development.
Help Us Shape It See How It Could Work

The Real Challenge

Financial information is everywhere. Understanding it is the hard part.

Whether you are a seasoned Chartered Accountant or a growing business owner, navigating numbers shouldn't feel like searching through a labyrinth of fragmented documents.

Spread Across Documents

Financial records are scattered across PDFs, bank statements, spreadsheets, ledgers, and portal downloads.

Difficult to Find Key Details

Crucial transactions or line items take precious minutes or hours to manually track down and cross-verify.

Statements Take Time

Interpreting complex balance sheets and profit & loss statements requires extensive manual calculation.

Tax Questions Need Research

GST, TDS, and statutory rules constantly evolve, requiring continuous referencing and contextual research.

Tedious Comparisons

Comparing periods and identifying unusual variance or discrepancies between quarters is highly repetitive.

Numbers Need Explanations

People need clear narrative explanations and verified evidence behind conclusions, not just raw isolated numbers.

The Concept

Imagine having an AI that understands your financial world.

We are exploring an AI assistant specifically built around Indian accounting, tax compliance, GST, financial statements, and financial document analysis.

Specialized Domains Being Explored

  • Accounting & Financial Statements: Balance sheets, Profit & Loss, trial balances, and ledgers.
  • Tax & GST Intelligence: GST returns, reconciliation, TDS guidelines, and tax document assistance.
  • Document Analysis: Invoices, bank statements, audit schedules, and Excel spreadsheets.
  • Financial Research: Searching accounting standards, compliance norms, and historical transaction patterns.
Core Design Philosophy

AI that assists judgment, never blindly replaces it.

"The AI assists with reading, searching, calculating, comparing, detecting and explaining. The professional remains in control of decisions and review."

We believe financial intelligence requires verification, context, and human oversight — never opaque automated guesses.

Built For Clarity

Built around the questions that matter.

Instead of simply storing financial information, the vision is to help you understand it.

01. Understand Documents

Ingest & Structure Multi-Format Files

Customer Value: Eliminates manual data re-entry and reading hours by instantly extracting tables and contextual balances from diverse formats.

Example: Upload a scanned balance sheet PDF and extract line-item totals in seconds.
02. Ask Questions

Natural Plain-Language Inquiries

Customer Value: Ask any question in plain English without writing complex database queries or navigating convoluted menus.

Example: "What were our total vendor travel expenses across Q2 and Q3?"
03. Analyze Financials

Instant Period & Margin Variance

Customer Value: Automatically compares quarters, computes ratios, and surfaces margin movements with zero formula setup.

Example: Highlight YoY gross margin compression and identify the contributing cost buckets.
04. Find What Needs Attention

Audit & Review Suggestions

Customer Value: Identifies potential anomalies, duplicate bills, and ledger mismatches early for human professional review.

Example: Flags two ₹85,000 invoices with the same vendor reference dated 2 days apart.
05. Understand GST & Tax

Compliance & Reconciliation Support

Customer Value: Clarifies complex statutory sections, reconciliation differences, and input tax credit (ITC) questions.

Example: Identify why GSTR-2B credit doesn't tally with your purchase ledger for July.
06. Explain the Why

Narrative Behind Every Figure

Customer Value: Gives leadership and clients clear written rationales behind figures, translating numbers into business context.

Example: Explain that operating profit dropped because administrative headcount expanded ahead of sales.
07. Generate Reports

Executive Summaries & Working Papers

Customer Value: Compiles verified findings, audit schedules, and management commentary into clean exportable documentation for clients, bankers, or stakeholders.

Deliverable Output: Generate client-ready review memos with tables, citations, and suggested audit questions.
