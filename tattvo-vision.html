PLANNED PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

Security, Privacy & Trust — Principles We Are Designing For

Financial data is among the most sensitive information an enterprise holds. We believe trust cannot be retrofitted—it must be architected into every layer from day one.

Core Foundation

"Your client's financial data should remain their data."

Tattvo is being designed around the principle that financial information is highly sensitive. The system should control who can access it, which client information can be retrieved, how AI can use the data, and how all activity is tracked.

Planned Data Boundary Journey
1. User & Firm Access Multi-factor verified identity
2. Isolated Tenant Client data strictly segregated
3. Controlled AI Retrieval Scoped ephemeral queries only
Principle 01

Client Data Isolation

A Chartered Accountant firm manages dozens or hundreds of independent corporate clients. A critical planned architectural pillar is strict, non-negotiable workspace isolation: Client A's data can never touch, leak into, or be referenced by Client B's workspace.

// Planned Workspace Architecture
CA FIRM WORKSPACE
│
├── CLIENT A (Pvt Ltd) [Isolated Storage & Vectors]
├── CLIENT B (LLP) [Isolated Storage & Vectors]
└── CLIENT C (Sole Prop) [Isolated Storage & Vectors]
CLIENT A DATA ≠ CLIENT B DATA

Zero Cross-Tenant Contamination

Document embeddings, financial ledgers, and conversational context are partitioned at the database and logical layer. An AI query inside Client A's environment cannot search, retrieve, or index records belonging to Client B.

Principle 02

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

In accounting and audit practices, different team members carry distinct legal and operational responsibilities. Tattvo's UI and permission layer are being planned around clear role hierarchies.

Firm Partner Tier 1

Sole authority for final audit sign-off, tax filing approvals, client workspace provisioning, and security settings.

Full Administrative & Approval Rights
CA / Audit Manager Tier 2

Conducts deep ratio analysis, reviews ledger anomalies, authors audit memos, and prepares compliance notes.

Deep Analysis & Memo Creation
Senior Accountant Tier 3

Uploads client financial statements, runs standard comparative variance checks, and reconciles bank statements.

Upload & Draft Reconciliation
Junior Accountant / Article Tier 4

Assigned to document intake and preliminary voucher extraction. Cannot view restricted partner memos or sign off on files.

Restricted Intake Scope
External Client / Business Owner (Read-Only) Client Portal

A dedicated portal view allowing the company founder or CFO to view published summaries, key ratios, and approved review memorandums without raw working audit notes.

Read-Only Finalized Reports
Principle 03

AI Data Control & Controlled Retrieval

How AI interacts with financial documents is the most common concern raised by business owners and professionals. Our planned architecture uses a controlled retrieval pipeline rather than indiscriminate model exposure.

No Public Model Training

Planned architecture principle: Customer financial records, ledger details, and private documents will never be fed into public foundation models for model retraining or public dataset pooling.

Scoped Retrieval Prompts

When a question is asked (e.g., "What was total depreciation?"), the system only retrieves the specific schedule needed to answer, minimizing raw context window exposure.

Ephemeral Query Context

Processing contexts are designed to be session-bound and discarded immediately after generating the verified answer and cited schedules.

Principle 04

Encryption: In Transit & At Rest

Data protection is treated as a fundamental baseline across both states of data life: when it is travelling over networks, and when it is stored on infrastructure.

In Transit Protection

All communication between the user's browser, mobile devices, and server endpoints is designed to travel through secure, encrypted communication channels to prevent packet inspection or interception.

At Rest Protection

Uploaded documents, extracted tables, and indexed financial ledgers are planned to reside in encrypted cloud storage volumes with segregated cryptographic keys managed at the tenant level.

Ingestion Gateway Boundary What does this mean?

Validates document file integrity, sanitizes uploaded metadata, scans for malicious payloads, and assigns cryptographically unique tenant authorization tokens before any financial parsing begins.

Logical Tenant Isolation What does this mean?

Ensures that database query planners and search indices enforce strict tenant-level row security, making it impossible for a query executed in one firm or client workspace to access data from another.

Deterministic Calculation Sandbox What does this mean?

Performs all math in a sterile, deterministic environment rather than relying on LLM token probabilities. Every sum, percentage variance, and ratio is computed via verified financial code algorithms.

Human Sign-off Checkpoint What does this mean?

Ensures that any output leaving the workspace—such as an audit report or tax memo—requires deliberate human confirmation and authorization, preserving legal and professional integrity.
